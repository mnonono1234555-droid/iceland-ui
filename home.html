A seventeen-year recipe for the original flavour.

Iceland opened its first counter in Erbil in 2009 with a simple promise: nothing frozen, nothing borrowed. Every patty is hand-pressed, every bun toasted to order, every sauce built in our own kitchen. Seventeen years later that promise hasn't changed — only the queue outside has grown longer.
